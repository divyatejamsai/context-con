## How it was run

- Openings: Crustdata Job Search, postings at companies of 10-5000 people, one per company and role. Staffing firms and job boards are excluded, since they post for clients and their own joiners aren't the hire.
- Real hire: someone whose job at that company started between 31 days before and 180 days after the posting date, in a role whose title matches the posting, and who was not already at the company (internal moves are excluded). Found with Crustdata Person Search on current and past employers. Openings with more than 5 matching joiners are dropped as ambiguous.
- Knowledge cutoff = posting date. Every person record Sourced sees is rewritten to how it read on that date: later jobs are removed (a start in the posting month counts as later), jobs that ended later are current again, the current title is recomputed, and a headline or summary naming a later employer is blanked. Search filters on the current job also match past jobs, and each row is re-checked against the original filter on its as-of record. Web search and fetch are off. The verifier and ranker run with today = posting date.
- The brief is the posting itself: title, company, location and the first 2,500 characters of the JD.
- Known leaks the data can't remove: location and skills are today's values, and candidates' employer records (headcount, funding) are today's.
- Reproduce: `cd backend && python -m evals.backtest all` (needs `CRUSTDATA_API_KEY`; `LLM_MODE=live` for Gemini brief parsing). Private per-person results stay in `backend/data/backtest/` (gitignored).

## Tuning: who is ready to move

Fit alone doesn't say who takes the job. Sourced's ranker now adds a capped likelihood-to-move bonus read from employment history alone: 1.5-4 years into the current role, at or past their usual tenure, between jobs, a one-level step up, the same work under a different title, and negatives for a very recent move or being overqualified. Weights were fitted by coordinate ascent on task-B MRR. The numbers below are **5-fold cross-validated on 44 openings**: every opening is scored with weights fitted only on the other folds.
